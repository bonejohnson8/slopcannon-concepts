An AI music video maker with room for your point of view.

What is Slopcannon?+

Slopcannon is an AI music video studio. Work with a conversational Producer to turn your audio, reference images, and ideas into a scene plan, generate video clips, and assemble a finished cut.

Can I use my own song and characters?+

Yes. Start with your audio and reference images, then tell your Producer how you want the video to feel. Use material you own or have permission to use.

How much control do I have?+

Review and edit the creative direction, scene descriptions, references, and performance choices. After generation, keep the takes you love and revise the scenes that need another pass.

When do I pay for generation?+

You review the generation cost before starting a run. The Producer helps you prepare your project first, so you can make an informed choice before using credits.
